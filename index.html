    VISION:

    An Experimental Study on the
    Invisible Structures of Emotion.

    We believe emotions are more than what we simply feel.

    Through KIRARA's music, sound interacts with the brain and senses, evoking unfamiliar emotional experiences that go beyond ordinary feelings.

    We seek to discover these hidden emotions, explore the structures behind them, and uncover the unique patterns formed through the interaction of sound and emotion.

    By translating these invisible experiences into visual forms, KIRARA : LAB aims to create new ways of perceiving, understanding, and experiencing emotions.

    RESEARCH FOCUS: EMOTION AS STRUCTURE OBSERVATION BEYOND DEFINITION TRANSLATION INTO VISUAL FORM
    PHILOSOPHY:

    On the Observationof InvisibleEmotional Structures.

    [RESEARCH STATEMENT]
    [ RESEARCHPURPOSE ]

    KIRARA : LAB investigates the emotional experiences evoked by KIRARA's music and their underlying neural responses.
    Through observation and analysis, we explore how sound stimulates the brain and shapes emotional experiences.

    Rather than treating emotions as predefined categories, we approach them as evolving experiences shaped by neural activity, sensory perception, and individual responses.

    Through KIRARA's music, we examine how sound stimulates the brain and nervous system, influences sensory perception, and triggers emotional responses. We observe how these responses interact, overlap, and evolve into complex emotional experiences.

    Each emotional state is analyzed through its internal relationships and distinctive patterns.

    We reorganize these characteristics into visual elements such as points, lines, forms, density, and movement, creating alternative representations of emotions that cannot be seen directly.
